ABOUT THE PROJECT

REPAIR / DECAY

An interactive music university project on the theme of upkeep, created by Tayla Kane, Shiya Wang, Jackson Moore, and Kanna Sudsawat

Repair / Decay is an interactive music experiment exploring what happens when a finished song is treated as something that can break down. Rather than existing as a single fixed audio file, the music is built from its individual stems — drums, bass, guitars, keys and vocals — and the listener becomes responsible for keeping those parts working.

THE IDEA

The system introduces random decay events while the song plays. Individual tracks can drift out of tune, become restricted by extreme EQ, or drift subtly in pitch. The breakdown is part of the music rather than an error outside of it.

THE LISTENER

Visitors can inspect individual tracks, hear them in isolation, see their history of decay and manually repair them. The project treats maintenance as part of listening — a finished work is not necessarily a stable work.

EVENTS

  • Detune — a track falls out of tune by a random number of cents.
  • High / low pass — a track is pushed into a narrow, megaphone-like frequency range.

IN DEVELOPMENT

The system is currently being developed as an interactive music work. More decay events, repair interactions, artwork and potentially multiple songs may be added as the project evolves.
